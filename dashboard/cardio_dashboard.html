Physics

Drum dia 250 mm + rope dia 38 mm → effective radius R_EFF = 0.144 m.
velocity = rpm·2π/60·R_EFF  ·  set_force = (resistance%/100)·MAX_FORCE_N  ·  power = force·velocity  ·  energy = ∫power·dt
MAX_FORCE = 7.5 kgf ≈ 73.6 N (configurable — edit the constants at the top of the page script).
"Set Force" is the commanded brake force, not a measured rope load (there is no force sensor on the rope).

CSV format — Auto workout

time_s, position, velocity_ms, resistance_pct, power_W

Auto workout logic

Arm the workout, then start pulling the rope. Resistance holds at 10 % until your first pull, then ramps linearly to 100 % over 2 min and holds there for the rest of the workout. During the hold, cadence is averaged over rolling 4 s windows: if it stays below 60 rpm for two consecutive windows the held load sheds 10 points (down to a 10 % floor) to let you get back up to speed, repeating until your cadence recovers — once you're back over 60 rpm the load simply holds at its reduced level. Every sample is logged; end the workout to auto-download the CSV.
