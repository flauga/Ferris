Physics

Drum dia 250 mm + rope dia 38 mm → effective radius R_EFF = 0.144 m.
velocity = rpm·2π/60·R_EFF  ·  set_force = (resistance%/100)·MAX_FORCE_N  ·  power = force·velocity  ·  energy = ∫power·dt
MAX_FORCE = 7.5 kgf ≈ 73.6 N (configurable — edit the constants at the top of the page script).
"Set Force" is the commanded brake force, not a measured rope load (there is no force sensor on the rope).

CSV format — Auto workout

time_s, position, velocity_ms, resistance_pct, power_W

Auto workout logic

Arm the workout, then start pulling the rope. Resistance holds at 10 % until your first pull, after which the load continuously adapts to your cadence, averaged over a single rolling 3 s window: below 60 rpm the load decreases rapidly, between 60–80 rpm it increases slowly, and above 80 rpm it increases rapidly — always clamped to a 10 % floor and 100 % ceiling. There is no fixed time-based ramp: how fast the load climbs is entirely driven by how fast you pedal. The rapid rate is ~0.75 %/s (the slow rate is gentler). All thresholds, the window length, and both rates are editable constants at the top of the page script. Every sample is logged; end the workout to auto-download the CSV.
